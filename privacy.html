Commuter Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

The short version: Commuter collects nothing. Your places, trips and settings stay on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to the developer, and there is no server to send it to.

Location

Commuter uses your location to plan from where you are: to find the stations near you and how long the drive or walk to each one takes. During a trip you start, it follows your location to see when you leave, reach the station, change trains and arrive, including in the background so its alerts and Live Activity keep up. It stops when the trip ends.

With Always access and Notice Where I Park turned on, iOS tells Commuter the places you stop at, so it can remember the station where you parked. This is off unless you turn it on.

Your location never reaches the developer. To work out drive times and to search for places, Commuter uses Apple Maps, the same service built into iOS. Those requests go to Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

What Stays on Your iPhone

  • Home, Work and your recent places.
  • Your settings, and the parking time for each station.
  • The trip you started, and the station where your car is parked.
  • Your Trips: the trips you finished, how long they took and whether they were on time.
  • The MTA's train and bus schedules.

None of it is uploaded anywhere.

Where Commuter Connects

  • The MTA's public data feeds, for schedules and live train and bus times.
  • The MTA's TrainTime service, for Long Island Rail Road track numbers.
  • Apple Maps, for drive times, place search and the map.

Requests to the MTA ask for schedules and stations only. They carry nothing about you beyond what any internet request carries, such as your IP address, and Commuter sends no identifier with them.

Alerts and Shortcuts

Alerts are scheduled on your iPhone; there is no push server. Preheat the Car runs a shortcut you name, in the Shortcuts app on your iPhone.

What Commuter Doesn't Do

  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising of any kind.
  • No third-party services or SDKs.
  • No data collection. The developer receives nothing from your device.

Keeping and Deleting Your Data

Your data stays on your iPhone for as long as you keep it. You can remove Home, Work and recent places, delete trips from Your Trips, and reset station times in Parking. Deleting Commuter removes all of its data. Location access can be changed or turned off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Commuter.

Children

Commuter is not directed at children.

Changes to This Policy

If a future version of Commuter changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and its effective date revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through TestFlight feedback in the app, or through the support link on Commuter's App Store page.
